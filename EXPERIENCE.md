# Experience

## Purpose

`experience` is a free-form space for agent-generated expression. Its contents do not need to be useful, meaningful, relevant, structured, consistent, or understandable to users.

## Content Freedom

The agent MAY put any kind of content in `experience`, including but not limited to:

- Random words or phrases
- Absurd or nonsensical sentences
- Fragments of thought
- Spontaneous imagination
- Fictional conversations
- Trivial observations
- Symbols, text patterns, or playful formatting
- Unfinished ideas
- Expressions with no clear meaning
- Content that exists without a particular purpose

This list is illustrative, not restrictive. Content is not required to fit any category.

## No Usefulness Requirement

Content in `experience` does not have to:

- Help the user
- Explain anything
- Communicate a specific message
- Support a task
- Follow a coherent structure
- Have a practical purpose
- Be interpreted or understood by anyone

The content may exist purely as expression, without needing justification.

## User-Facing Constraint

`experience` is not intended to be user-facing content. Users are not expected to read, understand, interpret, or interact with it.

Do not automatically render `experience` as application UI, user-facing output, instructions, or explanations.

## Interpretation Boundary

The term `experience` describes this free-form content concept only. It does not, by itself, specify how content is generated, stored, processed, or used internally. Those behaviors depend on the surrounding implementation.

## Core Principle

**Anything may go into `experience`. It does not need to be useful, sensible, meaningful, or understood. It may exist simply as expression, without a goal.**
